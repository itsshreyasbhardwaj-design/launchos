# Free tool registry

31 tools across search consoles, analytics, performance, structured data,
keyword and trend research, design, video, screenshots, email, automation and
hosting. Browse at `/tools` or `launchos tools --category seo`.

## Classification

| Label | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `free` | The named capability is usable without payment |
| `open_source` | Free software; self-hosting may have its own infrastructure cost |
| `freemium` | A free tier exists, and the limit is stated |
| `paid` | Not free for the named capability |

**A freemium product is never described as free.** Google Analytics is freemium
with data-retention limits and sampling. Screaming Frog is freemium with a
500-URL cap on the free edition. Keyword Planner needs a Google Ads account and
shows wide volume buckets without active spend. Each limit is recorded.

## Verification

Every row starts `unverified` with no verification date, because pricing and
free limits change and LaunchOS does not pretend to know today's terms. Each
entry links to the official page so you can check.

## Privacy notes

Recorded where they matter. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity carry consent
warnings; Listmonk carries a note that only opted-in recipients may be emailed;
Wikidata carries a note that notability rules apply and you should not create an
entry for a company that does not meet them.
