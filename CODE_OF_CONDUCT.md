# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason.
- Accept feedback on your work the same way.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience, different
  first languages, and different amounts of time.
- Keep discussion on the technical question at hand.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private
information, sustained disruption of discussion, or any conduct that would
reasonably make someone unwilling to participate.

## A project-specific note

LaunchOS refuses to do certain things — spam, fake endorsements, unauthorized
submission, search-engine manipulation, vote solicitation. Pull requests and
issues asking for these will be closed. That is a technical decision about
product boundaries, not a judgement of the person asking, and it will be
explained politely once.

## Reporting

Open an issue, or contact the maintainers privately through the repository's
security advisory form if it is sensitive. Reports will be handled discreetly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or reject contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently block anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate. Enforcement
will be proportionate, and a first incident will normally get a warning.
